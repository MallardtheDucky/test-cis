The line of authority

The Provincial Charter splits the state into a civil and a military branch. The military holds final authority during a declared security emergency, and the Permanent Emergency doctrine means that authority is never fully set aside. Select any box to open its ranks.

Reading the insignia

Every plate is a strip of triangles on a colored field. The field says which branch the wearer serves, the number of triangles says how senior, and the bars add the wearer’s qualifications.

Branch fields

    Triangle count

    One triangle is an entry grade. Each added triangle marks a step up, and the flag grades wear five or six. Orientation alternates along the strip, so the strip always reads as a continuous band.

    Bars

    A bar under or over the strip records a qualification. The color depends on the branch.

      The Supreme Commander plate

      Twelve triangles in two rows, gold over crimson, beside the Confederate hexagon. It is worn on the field of both the Navy and the Army, because the Supreme Commander commands both.

      Branch files

      Five services share one chain of command. Each has its own purpose, culture and field color.

      How the chain was built

      The command structure carries the logic of its history: a government that never agreed it had ended.

          THE CONTINUANCE

          It is a period of silence. Five centuries have passed since the Separatist Council fell on Mustafar, and the galaxy believes the Confederacy of Independent Systems died with it.

          It did not. Beneath the mountains of Osseriton, a hidden world in the Unknown Regions, Supreme Commander VEXIS, a Super Tactical Droid built from the mind of Admiral Trench, has kept the Confederate government alive, one unbroken session at a time.

          Under the doctrine of Absolute Concealment, the Provincial Authority has watched the Empire rise, the Rebellion triumph and the Yuuzhan Vong burn the Core, and has never once answered.

          Now the Republic has fallen into warlord states, and the order of the stars is coming apart. Vexis has judged that the age of concealment is ending.

          From the fleets of the Confederate Navy to the legions of the Army, from the quiet agents of CONFEDINTEL to the droid commanders of the Corps, the Provincial Authority prepares for the FINAL PHASE of the Clone Wars....
